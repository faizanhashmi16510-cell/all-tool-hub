GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are the tools free?

Yes. All 50 tools in this project are free to use. No account or subscription is required.

Are my files uploaded?

No. File operations are performed in your browser using client-side libraries. Your files are not sent to an AllToolsHub backend.

Do PDF and QR tools work?

Yes. PDF and QR utilities use browser-side libraries loaded from public CDNs. An internet connection is needed to load those libraries the first time.

Can I use this website on my phone?

Yes. The interface adapts to phones, tablets and desktop screens.
